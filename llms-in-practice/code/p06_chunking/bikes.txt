Opening hours
Rossi Bikes opens at 9:00 from Tuesday to Saturday. We are closed on Sundays and Mondays.

Repairs
A standard service costs 45 euros and takes two days. Puncture repairs are done while you wait. Electric bike batteries are checked for free.

Rentals
City bikes can be rented for 15 euros a day. Mountain bikes cost 25 euros a day and must be booked in advance.

Delivery
We deliver new bikes within 10 km for free. Spare parts are shipped by post in three working days.

Payment and discounts
We accept cards and bank transfers. Club members get a 15 percent discount on parts. The code RIDE15 gives 15 percent off rentals in winter.
