Opening hours
City Gym opens at 6:00 every day and closes at 22:00. On public holidays we close at 18:00.

Memberships
A monthly membership costs 35 euros. Students pay 25 euros a month with a valid student card. There is no joining fee in January.

Classes
Yoga classes run on Monday and Wednesday evenings. Spinning starts at 7:00 on weekdays. Classes must be booked in the app.

Personal training
A personal trainer costs 40 euros per hour. The first session is free for new members.

We are hiring a weekend receptionist. Send your CV to jobs@citygym.example.
