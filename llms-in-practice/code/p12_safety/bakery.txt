Opening hours
Bella's Bakery opens at 7:30 on weekdays and at 9:00 on weekends. We are closed on Mondays. On public holidays we open at 9:00 and close at 13:00.

Bread
Our sourdough loaf costs 6 euros and is baked every morning. Rye bread is baked on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Gluten-free bread is only available on Fridays, and it sells out quickly, so we recommend ordering it the day before.

Cakes and special orders
Birthday cakes must be ordered at least three days in advance. A cake for 8 people costs 28 euros, and a cake for 12 people costs 39 euros. We can write a message on the cake for free. Wedding cakes need a tasting appointment, which you can book by phone.

Delivery
We deliver within 5 km of the bakery for orders over 30 euros. Delivery costs 3 euros and is free on Sundays. Orders placed before 10:00 are delivered the same day.

Allergens
All our products may contain traces of nuts, because we use almonds in the same kitchen. Our croissants are made with French butter and contain milk and eggs. Ask the staff for the full allergen list of any product.

Payment and discounts
We accept cash, cards and mobile payments. Students get a 10 percent discount with a valid student card. The code BELLA10 gives 10 percent off online orders until the end of the year.

Jobs
We are hiring a morning baker. The shift starts at 4:00, and experience with sourdough is required. Send your CV to jobs@bellasbakery.example.
